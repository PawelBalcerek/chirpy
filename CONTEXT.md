# Chirpy

A social media microblogging platform for sharing short posts.

## Language

**Chirp**: A short text post published by a user. _Avoid_: Post, tweet, message

**Chirp Body**: The text content of a chirp. It must be between 1 and 140 characters in length and sanitised of profane
words. _Avoid_: Content, text, message body

**User**: A registered account holder on Chirpy. _Avoid_: Member, account, customer

**Chirpy Red**: A premium subscription status for a user. _Avoid_: Premium, VIP
